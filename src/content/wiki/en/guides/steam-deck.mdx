## Does Dawn of War IV work on Steam Deck?

**Possibly — but unofficially.** Dawn of War IV is a Windows (PC) title targeting Windows 11 officially. However, the developer has confirmed reports from Closed Beta players who **successfully ran the game on Linux** — and Steam Deck's compatibility layer is Linux-based. That is a promising signal, not a promise: neither Steam Deck nor Linux is officially supported, and compatibility with future updates is not guaranteed. Official Steam Deck verification status has not been published — this page updates the moment it is.

## What are the risks of playing on Linux or Deck?

- **Update breakage** — a game patch or Proton change can break an unofficial setup at any time
- **Anti-cheat / multiplayer** — verify how [1v1–3v3 multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/) behaves under Proton before ranked play
- **Performance** — the game asks for a [GTX 1660 at minimum](/guides/system-requirements/); the Deck's GPU sits below that bar, so expect lowered settings in big battles
- **No support tickets** — official channels will point you back to Windows

## The confirmed handheld path: GeForce NOW

**GeForce NOW support is officially confirmed** for launch — the cleanest way to play Dawn of War IV on a handheld (or any weak machine): stream it from your Steam library with remote hardware doing the work. Input and stability are on you, but the [125 GB install](/guides/download-size/) and the [GPU requirements](/guides/system-requirements/) stop mattering entirely.

## What about Windows 10?

Runs, unofficially — same category as Linux. The developers confirmed it works but list [Windows 11 in the requirements](/guides/system-requirements/) because Windows 10 is past Microsoft's support window. If your machine handled the Closed Beta, it handles launch.

## Bottom line for Deck players

- Want it **guaranteed**? GeForce NOW on the Deck — confirmed
- Want it **native**? Wait for official verification or launch-week community reports (this page will carry them)
- Playing on a proper PC instead? The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) has your first ten hours mapped, and [release times](/guides/release-time/) has exactly when the [December 3 unlock](/guides/editions-preorder/) hits.
