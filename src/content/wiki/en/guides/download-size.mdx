## How big is the Dawn of War IV download?

**Around 100 GB installed, 125 GB listed officially.** Per the publisher's storage clarification: the install is approximately 100 GB, **including around 25 GB of cutscenes**, plus highly detailed units and assets you can zoom in on. The official requirement of **125 GB available space** reserves additional room for future updates and DLC beyond day one — not a bigger initial download.

## Why does an RTS need 100 GB?

Three confirmed contributors:

1. **~25 GB of cutscenes** — the four [campaign](/guides/campaign-overview/) storylines ship with substantial cinematic production
2. **High-detail units** — the [64-unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/) is built to hold up when you zoom to street level, including faction ultimates like the [Imperial Knights](/guides/ultimate-units/)
3. **Four factions of assets** — [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/), [Orks](/factions/orks/), [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) and [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) each carry full visual kits

## Can I pre-load Dawn of War IV?

**Pre-load is live from November 27** for all editions. See [release times](/guides/release-time/) for your local unlock hour. Practical advice regardless: **clear 125 GB before December 3** so unlock day is play, not delete-things-hours.

## What if my SSD is too small?

Options in order of pain:

- Free space — 125 GB is the safe number; ~105 GB realistically for day one
- Install on a second drive — an HDD install works but hurts load times in [multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/)
- **GeForce NOW** — confirmed at launch, streams the game with no local install at all (see [system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/))

## Does the size grow after launch?

Likely, and that is deliberate: the [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/) ships two story expansions, free map packs and a Mission Editor — the 125 GB figure already budgets headroom for them. Plan capacity for the year, not just day one. For what hardware you will need alongside the space, see the full [system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/) breakdown.
