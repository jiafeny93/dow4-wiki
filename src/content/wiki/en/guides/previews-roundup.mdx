## Hands-on previews: the faction buzz

The standout preview takeaway: **Adeptus Mechanicus "plays very differently" from every other faction** — the recurring line from hands-on coverage, and the reason veterans are eyeing it as the high-skill pick ([faction](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/), [tier list](/guides/faction-tier-list/)). Coverage has also centered the [four asymmetric factions](/guides/factions-overview/), the return of [The Last Stand](/guides/last-stand/), and [base building](/guides/base-building/) back after DoW2's experiment.
