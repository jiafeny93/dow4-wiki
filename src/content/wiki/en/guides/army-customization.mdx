## Does Dawn of War IV have army customization?

**Yes — expanded.** Official pre-launch information confirms **expanded army customization options** in Dawn of War IV: the system for making your army yours, deeper than previous entries. The full option list (color schemes, banners, chapter liveries) gets documented here the moment the game unlocks November 30 — every page on this site carries a last-verified date.

## What is army customization in Dawn of War?

The series' beloved ritual: paint your [army](/guides/base-building/) in your colors. Historically that has meant:

- **Army color schemes** — primary, trim, weapon and eye colors across your whole roster
- **Chapter/banner identity** — fight as your own successor chapter rather than a stock one
- **Visible everywhere** — your colors ride into [multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/), replays and [The Last Stand](/guides/last-stand/)

For a faction like the [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) — with the [Blood Ravens and Dark Angels](/guides/characters/) in the campaign — custom chapter colors are practically lore. ORKS get klan paint, [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) dynasties, [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) forge-world liveries: every faction has a painting tradition to draw from.

## How deep does customization go?

Confirmed: expanded options. Unverified until launch: exact editor depth (per-unit war-gear? banner designer? preset sharing?). The community's wishlist — built on the [official trailers](/guides/trailers/) — runs toward a full army painter; we will document precisely what ships, no speculation presented as fact.

## Does customization affect gameplay?

Cosmetic identity, not power — that is the series' design lineage. Your skill with the [64-unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/) and [faction counters](/guides/faction-tier-list/) decides matches; customization decides whether enemies remember **who** beat them.

## Where do I show off my army?

Everywhere armies appear — 30 [multiplayer maps](/guides/game-modes/), [co-op campaign](/guides/coop-campaign/) playthroughs, and [Last Stand](/guides/last-stand/) survival runs. Set your colors early in the [beginner path](/guides/beginner-guide/), then take the field on [December 3](/guides/release-time/) looking exactly how you want.
