## Will Dawn of War IV have mods?

**Partially confirmed — through official tools.** The published [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/) includes a free **Mission Editor**: create custom missions, play them, and **share them** with other players. That is a creator pipeline built into the game, not a bolted-on afterthought.

## What exactly is the Mission Editor?

Per the official roadmap: a toolset to build custom missions for the game — think new scenarios on Dawn of War IV's [64-unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/) and four factions, shareable with the community. It ships free in Year One (no exact date yet — [announcements](/guides/dlc-expansions/) are tracked here same-day). What the editor's ceiling is (scripts? maps? rules?) will be documented once it lands.

## What about traditional mods?

**Unannounced.** File-level modding, Steam Workshop integration, total conversions — none of that is on the official record. The honest framing:

- **Confirmed**: Mission Editor with sharing (Year One, free)
- **Not confirmed**: Workshop support, SDK, or anything beyond the editor
- **History lesson**: the series has a deep modding heritage (the Unification mod for older titles still draws players today) — if DoW4 opens that door even partially, the community will walk through it

## Why custom content matters even if you never mod

- **Free missions forever** — shared creations extend the game beyond the [70+ campaign missions](/guides/campaign-overview/)
- **Co-op fuel** — custom missions + [two-player co-op](/guides/coop-campaign/) = endless replay with a friend
- **Longevity signal** — a developer shipping creator tools in year one is investing in the game's [long tail](/guides/developer/)

## When can I start?

The editor arrives post-launch during Year One. Until then: the [gameplay systems](/guides/gameplay/), [The Last Stand](/guides/last-stand/) survival and [multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/) are the playground — from December 3 (or November 30 with the [Commander Edition](/guides/editions-preorder/); [release times](/guides/release-time/)).
