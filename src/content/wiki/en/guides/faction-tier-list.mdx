## Why Space Marines are the safest launch pick?

Every layer of their kit is documented: [durable elite infantry](/factions/space-marines/), named heroes with clear roles, and the Imperial Knights ultimate as a comeback mechanism (see [ultimate units](/guides/ultimate-units/)). For launch-week ladder — when everyone is learning — a faction that survives mistakes wins games by default. Start with the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) path if this is your pick.

## Why not wait for Necrons or Adeptus Mechanicus?

Both could be secretly strong — Necrons' [attrition identity](/factions/necrons/) and [Mechanicus' unconventional mechanics](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) are exactly the kind of designs that dominate once mastered. But with the least revealed information, they carry the most launch-week risk. High reward, high unknowns.

## How will this tier list change after launch?

The moment Commander Edition unlocks on **November 30**, real data starts flowing: unit stats land in our [sortable unit table](/guides/unit-roster/), and this page gets its first balance-based revision. The full meta tier list — with matchup notes for [1v1 to 3v3 multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/) — follows during launch week, updated per [balance patch](/guides/roadmap/).

## Is faction choice permanent?

No. Every faction is unlocked for [multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/) and each has its own [campaign](/guides/campaign-overview/) storyline — playing all four is the intended journey. The tier list answers only "where do I start on day one", not "what is locked". For team play, everything supports [two-player co-op](/guides/co-op-guide/).
