## What is the Dawn of War IV Painter Demo?

A **free standalone demo** on Steam letting you customize your armies ahead of the December 3 launch. It features the most powerful Painter in Dawn of War history, with units from all [four factions](/guides/factions-overview/) available for customization.

## How do Painter codes work?

The Painter Demo lets you **share your creations using unique sharing codes**. Other players can import your designs and use them in their own armies. Here is the flow:

1. Design your scheme in the [Painter Demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2272360/)
2. Save your design
3. Generate a **share code**
4. Share the code with friends (or the community)
5. They import the code and get your exact colour scheme

This is the system behind the "painter codes" and "scheme codes" searches appearing pre-launch.

## Basic Painter vs Advanced Painter

| | Basic Painter | Advanced Painter |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Army-wide colour schemes | Individual armour & body parts |
| Detail level | Faction colours | Down to the smallest component |
| Customisation | Primary / trim / weapons colours | Colours, materials, patterns, decals, weathering |
| Best for | Quick unique looks | Battle-hardened veterans vs. pristine wargear |

## Can I import my designs into the full game?

**Yes.** Designs saved in the Painter Demo **import into the full game** when Dawn of War IV launches on [December 3](/guides/release-time/). Everything you create now carries over — start designing your army before the war for [Kronus](/guides/kronus/) begins.

## Which factions are in the Painter Demo?

All four launch factions: [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/), [Orks](/factions/orks/), [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) and [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/). Every [unit in the 64-unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/) is available for customization.

## Is the Painter Demo free?

Yes — completely free on Steam, no purchase required. It is a standalone tool, not a game demo; you are customizing armies, not playing matches. See also the full [army customization guide](/guides/army-customization/) for the in-game system.

## How do I get the Painter Demo?

Search for "Dawn of War IV Painter Demo" on Steam, or visit the official [Dawn of War IV Steam page](/guides/preorder-price/) and look for the demo section. [Pre-load](/guides/download-size/) for the full game is also live from November 27.
