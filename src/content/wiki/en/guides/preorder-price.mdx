## How much does Dawn of War IV cost?

**$59.99** for the standard edition on Steam — pre-orders are live now. The [Commander Edition](/guides/editions-preorder/) tiers up from there with bonuses headlined by the **three-day early unlock on November 30**. There is no free-to-play version, and no [Game Pass or console release](/guides/consoles-gamepass-mac/) exists.

## Where should I buy Dawn of War IV?

**Steam — the official listing.** It is the only verified storefront at launch:

- The game is PC/Steam only ([platforms explained](/guides/consoles-gamepass-mac/))
- Pre-load opens closer to launch ([release guide](/guides/release-time/)) — clear **125 GB** first ([download size](/guides/download-size/))
- Your PC must clear the [official system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/)

### What about CD key sites?

**Beware.** Third-party "dow 4 cd key" resellers surface around every launch. Risks: region-locked or revoked keys, zero refund path, and no recourse when a patch bricks an unsupported activation. A $5 saving on a game you'll play for [a year of expansions](/guides/roadmap/) is poor math. Buy where support exists.

## Is there a pre-order bonus?

Pre-order incentives flow through the official Steam listing — the headline split is:

| Edition | What you get | When you play |
|---|---|---|
| Standard — $59.99 | Full game | December 3 |
| Commander Edition | Bonuses + **3-day early unlock** | **November 30** |

Full comparison in [editions & early access](/guides/editions-preorder/). Pre-ordering is not required for anything gameplay-critical — the entire game, all four factions, every mode is in the box on day one ([gameplay overview](/guides/gameplay/)).

## GeForce NOW buyers

Buying on Steam is doubly smart if you plan to [stream via GeForce NOW](/guides/system-requirements/) — it streams from your Steam library, so the same purchase covers both native and cloud play.

## The checklist before December 3

1. Buy on Steam (this page's advice stands for a reason)
2. Clear [125 GB](/guides/download-size/) on an SSD
3. Check your [specs](/guides/system-requirements/)
4. Skim the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) while it downloads
5. Watch the [release time page](/guides/release-time/) for your local unlock hour
