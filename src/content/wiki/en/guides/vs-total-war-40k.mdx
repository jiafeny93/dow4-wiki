## Which should you buy: Dawn of War IV or Total War 40K?

**Short answer: they are not fighting for the same slot — but if you want 40K strategy this year, Dawn of War IV is the only one that exists.** Total War: Warhammer 40,000 was officially announced in December 2025 by SEGA and Creative Assembly with **no release date**; Wikipedia lists 2027 and the developers call it a long-term effort. Dawn of War IV ships **December 3, 2026**, pre-orders live at [$59.99](/guides/preorder-price/).

## Gameplay: two different 40K fantasies

**Dawn of War IV** is the intimate war: build a base, command squads, take territory, and zoom to street level while [Imperial Knights](/guides/ultimate-units/) tear through the line — [glory kills](/guides/glory-kills/) and all. **Total War 40K** will be the strategic war: the series' formula of turn-based empire management exploding into battles with thousands of units. If you know Total War: Warhammer's fantasy trilogy, you know the shape.

## Release-date reality check

- **DoW4**: locked — December 3, 2026, with a [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/) already published
- **TW40K**: Creative Assembly's own words — "not sharing a release date at this time"; community windows run 2027; details promised "later in 2026" ([announcement](https://www.totalwar.com/news/total-war-warhammer-40000))

## The verdict for buyers

1. **Want 40K strategy now** → Dawn of War IV, no contest — check [specs](/guides/system-requirements/) and [release times](/guides/release-time/)
2. **Want the Total War scale** → wait — nothing to pre-order exists for TW40K
3. **Most 40K fans** → realistically both, a year apart — start with the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) now, and our [vs DoW3](/guides/vs-dow3/) for why this return to roots matters
