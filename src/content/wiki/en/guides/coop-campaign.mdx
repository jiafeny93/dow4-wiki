## Does Dawn of War IV have a co-op campaign?

**Yes.** The entire Dawn of War IV campaign — all four faction storylines, **more than 70 missions** — supports **two-player co-op**. You and a friend play the same non-linear campaign together, from the [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) and [Orks](/factions/orks/) storylines to the [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) and [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/). See the full [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/) for structure, or the general [co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/) for every co-op mode.

## How does campaign co-op work in Dawn of War IV?

The confirmed pattern: one player hosts the campaign session, the second joins via invite, and you fight the same missions as a duo. Both players command on the same battlefield — classic Dawn of War team play, now woven through the whole story. Exact details on progression carryover and save sharing will be verified at launch and updated here (every page on this site carries a last-verified date).

## Which campaign should co-op pairs play first?

- **Story-first pairs** — [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/): the Blood Ravens and Dark Angels storyline is the series' emotional core
- **Aggressive pairs** — [Orks](/factions/orks/): swarm tempo punishes coordinated aggression beautifully
- **Challenge pairs** — Necrons or [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/): the least revealed rosters, the most discovery

New to the series? Start with the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) path first.

## Is co-op campaign separate from multiplayer?

No separate mode — co-op is built into everything. Beyond the campaign, two-player co-op extends to [skirmish against AI](/guides/game-modes/), team formats, and [The Last Stand](/guides/last-stand/) survival (three players there). The competitive side — 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 — is covered in [game modes](/guides/game-modes/).

## Does co-op cost extra or need a second copy?

Each player needs their own copy of the game; co-op itself is included at every edition level. Edition differences only affect bonuses like the [Commander Edition's three-day early unlock](/guides/editions-preorder/) starting November 30 — see [release times](/guides/release-time/) for exactly when you can start, and [crossplay](/guides/crossplay/) for the platform situation (PC-only at launch).
