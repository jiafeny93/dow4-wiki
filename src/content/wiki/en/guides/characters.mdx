## Who are the main characters in Dawn of War IV?

The confirmed named cast so far centers on the [Space Marine campaign](/factions/space-marines/) on [Kronus](/guides/kronus/):

| Character | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Cyrus** | Sniper Captain, Blood Ravens veteran | Confirmed |
| **Jonah Orion** | Librarian — the Blood Ravens' psychic mainstay | Confirmed |
| **Lion El'Jonson** | Primarch of the Dark Angels | Confirmed — campaign appearance |
The [Dark Angels](/factions/space-marines/) field their own captain alongside the Blood Ravens — searches for "dawn of war 4 dark angels captain" reflect the campaign's dual-chapter structure, though the specific captain's name is not yet revealed.

| **Thothmek the Chronomancer** | Primary Necron commander ([Necrons](/factions/necrons/)) | Confirmed — also the [Tacticus crossover character](/guides/tacticus-codes/) |
| **Ahmnok the Skorpekh Lord** | Second Necron commander | Confirmed |
| **Jan Theysen** | [Game Director](/guides/developer/) (appears in official Battlefield Reports, not an in-game character) | Meta |

## Will Gorgutz return?

The most-asked legacy question (searches for "dow 4 gorgutz" spike whenever [Kronus](/guides/kronus/) comes up — the Ork warboss was Dark Crusade's icon). **His return is not confirmed.** The [Ork campaign](/factions/orks/) has its own storyline; whether the old warboss fronts it will be verified at launch. This page updates the moment it is — no rumors presented as fact.

## What about the other factions' leaders?

The [Necron](/factions/necrons/) campaign leads ARE named — Thothmek and Ahmnok above. The [Ork](/factions/orks/) and [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) campaign leads — their [ultimate units](/guides/ultimate-units/) (the Awakened Monolith, the Stompa) headline their rosters instead. Named-commander reveals land in official trailers and [updates](/guides/roadmap/); we document them with sources as they arrive.

## Who voices the cast?

Confirmed so far: **Jonathan Keeble** — the narrator/announcer voice of the [official Game Overview Trailer](/guides/gameplay/), a voice Warhammer audiobook fans know well. The wider voice cast (including any [Lion El'Jonson voice actor](/factions/space-marines/)) has not been officially listed — IMDB-style aggregations are unverified until the credits roll December 3.

## Where do these characters fight?

Across four interlocking storylines on **Kronus**, 70+ missions, solo or [two-player co-op](/guides/coop-campaign/) — the [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/) has the structure, and the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) has your first steps alongside them.
