## How many factions are in Dawn of War IV?

Four: **Space Marines, Orks, Necrons and Adeptus Mechanicus**. All four are playable in multiplayer, skirmish and — unusually for the series — each gets its own non-linear campaign storyline within the 70+ mission campaign. The game also supports two-player co-op in every mode (see the [co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/)).

## Which faction should I pick?

| Faction | Style in one line | Best for | Ultimate unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) | Durable elite infantry, strong heroes | Beginners, campaign fans | Imperial Knights |
| [Orks](/factions/orks/) | Cheap swarms, relentless aggression | Tempo players | Stompa |
| [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) | Slow, undying attrition | Long-game strategists | Awakened Monolith |
| [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) | Unconventional mechanics | Veterans wanting something new | TBA |

For your very first hours with the game — regardless of faction — start with the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/).

## What are ultimate units?

Each faction caps its tech tree with a faction-defining **ultimate unit** — super-heavies that can swing whole matches:

- **Imperial Knights** — towering Space Marine walkers
- **Stompa** — the Orks' rolling fortress
- **Awakened Monolith** — the Necrons' awakened citadel

All three are compared in depth in the [ultimate units guide](/guides/ultimate-units/). Adeptus Mechanicus' ultimate has not been detailed yet; it will be verified and added at launch.

## How big is each faction's roster?

Around **64 multiplayer-relevant units across all factions at launch** (roughly 110 including campaign-only units), with every revealed unit tracked in the [complete unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/). Stats pages go live with verified data when the game unlocks December 3 — Commander Edition owners start three days early on November 30 ([release times](/guides/release-time/), [editions compared](/guides/editions-preorder/)).

## Does faction choice lock the campaign?

No. Each faction storyline is its own campaign — you play the campaign of the faction you pick, and can play them all. The structure is explained in the [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/). Between campaigns, multiplayer and The Last Stand co-op survival (see [game modes](/guides/game-modes/) and the [Last Stand guide](/guides/last-stand/)), there is a full game on every side of the faction picker.
