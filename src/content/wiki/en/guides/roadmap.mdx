## What is planned after Dawn of War IV launches?

KING Art and Deep Silver have published a **Year One roadmap** mixing paid expansions with free updates. The revised roadmap (updated after the game moved from September 17 to December 3, 2026) currently includes:

| Content | Type | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| The Blood Ravens Story Prologue | Paid expansion | Year One |
| AFTERMATH | Paid expansion | Year One |
| New modes | Free update | Year One |
| Map Pack 1 | Free update | Year One |
| Mission Editor (create / play / share) | Free update | Year One |
| Crusade mode | Free update | Early 2027 (moved from autumn 2026) |

## What are the two paid expansions?

- **The Blood Ravens Story Prologue** — story content tied to the series' signature chapter; see the [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/) for how the Blood Ravens fit the launch storyline
- **AFTERMATH** — the second announced expansion; details still limited

## What is the Mission Editor?

A free Year One update adding **create, play and share custom missions** — a long-requested feature for the community to build its own content, with obvious cooperative potential (see the [co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/)). Custom mission curation and highlights will get their own pages here once the editor ships.

## What is Crusade mode?

Crusade — the series' narrative army-progression mode — returns as a **free update in early 2027**. It was originally planned for autumn 2026 and moved with the launch delay. When it arrives, its army-building rules will be documented with the same verified-data standard as everything else on this site.

## Why does the roadmap matter to players?

It signals post-launch support — the exact question that haunted Dawn of War III, which was effectively abandoned. A published Year One plan with both story DLC and free systems (maps, editor, modes) is the publisher's commitment that this game has a runway. For launch-day content, see [release times](/guides/release-time/), [editions](/guides/editions-preorder/) and [game modes](/guides/game-modes/); balance patches for the [unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/) will be tracked here as they land.
