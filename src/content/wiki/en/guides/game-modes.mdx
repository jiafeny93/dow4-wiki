## What game modes does Dawn of War IV have?

Five pillars at launch:

1. **Competitive multiplayer** — 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3
2. **Skirmish vs AI** — up to five AI opponents, four game types
3. **The Last Stand** — the fan-favorite co-op survival mode, returning
4. **Campaign** — four non-linear faction storylines, 70+ missions ([campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/))
5. **Custom / co-op everything** — campaign and all modes playable in two-player co-op ([co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/))

## How does multiplayer work?

Competitive multiplayer supports **1v1, 2v2 and 3v3** at launch across **30 maps**. Faction picks come from the four launch sides — [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/), [Orks](/factions/orks/), [Necrons](/factions/necrons/), [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) — each capped by a faction-defining ultimate unit (see [ultimate units](/guides/ultimate-units/)). Map-by-map pages with callouts and resource layouts will be published under the maps category after launch.

## What are the four skirmish game types?

Skirmish mode lets you face up to **five AI opponents** in four confirmed game types:

| Type | Objective style |
|---|---|
| Annihilation | Destroy the enemy base |
| Domination | Hold territory for victory points |
| Maelstrom | Shifting objective control |
| Defend | Hold out against attacking waves |

Skirmish is the recommended training ground before ranked multiplayer — the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) suggests a learning path through it.

## What is The Last Stand?

The mode series fans have been begging for: a **co-op survival mode** where a small team holds out against escalating waves, growing a hero between rounds. It returns in Dawn of War IV — the full breakdown is in our [Last Stand guide](/guides/last-stand/).

## Is there a single-player campaign?

Yes — four of them. Each faction has its own non-linear storyline, 70+ missions in total, fully playable solo or in two-player co-op. Structure and mission breakdown in the [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/).

December 3, 2026 on PC via Steam — Commander Edition owners get a three-day early unlock on November 30.
