## Is Dawn of War IV better than Dawn of War 3?

Honest pre-launch answer: **by design intent, it is a deliberate correction of everything DoW3 got wrong.** The final verdict lands December 3. What is already verifiable is the philosophy shift — table below.

## Dawn of War IV vs Dawn of War 3: the comparison

| | Dawn of War 3 (2017) | Dawn of War IV (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Developer | Relic Entertainment | [KING Art Games](/guides/developer/) |
| Campaign | Single storyline, hero-focused | **Four non-linear campaigns, 70+ missions** ([guide](/guides/campaign-overview/)) |
| Co-op | Limited | **Two-player co-op in every mode** ([co-op campaign](/guides/coop-campaign/)) |
| The Last Stand | Absent | **Returned** — 3 players, 20 waves ([guide](/guides/last-stand/)) |
| Campaign setting | New front | **[Kronus](/guides/kronus/)** — the Dark Crusade homeland |
| Iconic factions | Space Marines, Orks + Eldar | Space Marines, Orks + [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) and [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) |
| Elite-hero focus | Central design pillar | Rebalanced toward armies with faction ultimates ([units](/guides/ultimate-units/)) |
| Post-launch plan | Effectively abandoned | **Published Year One roadmap** ([DLC guide](/guides/dlc-expansions/)) |
| Multiplayer | 3 formats | 1v1 / 2v2 / 3v3 + 4 skirmish types across 30 maps ([modes](/guides/game-modes/)) |

## What stayed the same?

The good bones: base-and-army RTS fundamentals, [glory-kill flair in combat](/guides/glory-kills/), Games Workshop's universe license, and the Blood Ravens thread running from the very first game ([campaign](/guides/campaign-overview/)).

## Which should a new player get?

Dawn of War IV, unambiguously, if choosing today: modern [unit detail](/guides/unit-roster/), every-mode co-op, a published support plan, and $59.99 on [Steam](/guides/preorder-price/). DoW3 remains historically interesting — that is all. New to the series?
