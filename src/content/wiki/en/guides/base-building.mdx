## Does Dawn of War IV have base building?

**Yes.** Dawn of War IV plays the classic RTS loop: construct your base, raise an army, take and hold territory, break the enemy. It is a deliberate return to the series' foundations — [four asymmetric factions](/guides/factions-overview/) with full tech trees capped by [ultimate units](/guides/ultimate-units/), fought across [30 multiplayer maps](/guides/game-modes/).

## How does it differ from Dawn of War 2?

Sharply. Dawn of War 2 stripped bases and armies down to a handful of hero squads — a design that split the fanbase. Dawn of War IV reverses course:

| | Dawn of War 2 | Dawn of War IV |
|---|---|---|
| Bases | Minimal / none | Full base construction |
| Army size | Small hero squads | Armies from a [64-unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/) |
| Focus | RPG-lite squads | Classic RTS economy + territory |
| Heroes | The core | Strong but embedded ([Cyrus, Jonah Orion](/guides/characters/)) |

For the full generation-over-generation picture including DoW3, see [Dawn of War IV vs DoW3](/guides/vs-dow3/).

## How does a base-building match actually flow?

1. **Open** — economy setup, first map points ([beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/))
2. **Mid** — tech choices and [unit counters](/guides/unit-roster/) from your faction's roster
3. **Late** — ultimate-unit showdowns: [Imperial Knights](/guides/ultimate-units/), the Stompa, the Awakened Monolith
4. **Throughout** — [glory kills](/guides/glory-kills/) punctuate the fighting; objectives vary by [game mode](/guides/game-modes/) (Annihilation, Domination, Maelstrom, Defend)

## Is base building in co-op and campaign too?

Yes — the [co-op campaign](/guides/coop-campaign/) and every mode share the same systems; you and a partner can divide base and army duties in the [70+ mission campaign](/guides/campaign-overview/) on [Kronus](/guides/kronus/).

## Is it beginner-friendly with full bases?

Yes — [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) are the classic forgiving start, skirmish AI scales, and the [faction tier list](/guides/faction-tier-list/) ranks launch-week picks. PC specs are modest for an RTS this detailed — [official requirements](/guides/system-requirements/) start at a GTX 1660, and [GeForce NOW](/guides/system-requirements/) removes even that bar before [December 3](/guides/release-time/).
