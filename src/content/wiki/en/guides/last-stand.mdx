## What is The Last Stand in Dawn of War IV?

The Last Stand is the co-op survival mode introduced in Dawn of War II and now officially returning in Dawn of War IV. A small team of players each controls a single hero, defending a holdout map against escalating waves of enemies, with upgrades and gear decisions between rounds. It became one of the most beloved corners of the franchise — its return was a headline feature of the Dawn of War IV announcement.

## How does The Last Stand work?

The classic loop, expected to carry over:

1. Pick a hero with a distinct kit
2. Survive timed waves of increasing difficulty
3. Earn upgrade currency between waves
4. Spend it on gear and abilities that compound your build
5. Push your team's best round count higher each run

Wave-by-wave breakdowns and hero loadout pages will be published here the week of launch, verified in-game — every page on this site carries a last-verified date.

## Can I play The Last Stand solo or co-op?

The Last Stand is built around co-op — and in Dawn of War IV, **every mode in the game supports two-player co-op**, including the campaign (see the [co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/)). Team composition tips arrive with launch-week data.

## Why does The Last Stand matter for a Dawn of War game?

It outlived the multiplayer of the game it shipped in. Dawn of War II's Last Stand ran for years and kept players in the ecosystem long after competitive play moved on. For Dawn of War IV it fills the same role: the low-pressure, high-replayability mode between [campaign](/guides/campaign-overview/) runs and [multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/) sessions. For newcomers it is also a gentle on-ramp — see the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/).

## When is The Last Stand playable?

At launch: December 3, 2026, with the Commander Edition's three-day early unlock starting November 30 ([release times](/guides/release-time/)). Heroes draw from the four launch factions — [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/), [Orks](/factions/orks/), [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) and [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) — and any heroes added post-launch will follow the [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/).
