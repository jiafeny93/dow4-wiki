## The 6 gameplay systems that matter

**1. Four asymmetric factions.** [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) (durable elites), [Orks](/factions/orks/) (swarm tempo), [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) (undying attrition) and [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) (officially "plays very differently"). Roster details live in the [sortable unit table](/guides/unit-roster/).

**2. Glory kills.** The signature execution mechanic — weakened enemies finished with cinematic kills woven into combat tempo. Rewards and exact triggers get verified at launch ([glory kills deep dive](/guides/glory-kills/)).

**3. Ultimate units.** Each faction's tech-tree capstone: [Imperial Knights](/guides/ultimate-units/), the Ork Stompa, the Necron Awakened Monolith. Match-swinging super-heavies.

**4. Co-op everywhere.** Two-player co-op runs through the whole [70+ mission campaign](/guides/campaign-overview/), every [game mode](/guides/game-modes/), and [The Last Stand](/guides/last-stand/) survival — three commanders, 20 waves, strike-force companions.

**5. Four skirmish formats + ranked.** Annihilation, Domination, Maelstrom (objectives appearing across the map) and Defend, versus up to five AI — then 1v1/2v2/3v3 [multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/) across 30 launch maps.

**6. Post-launch depth.** A published [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/): two story expansions, free map packs, a create-and-share Mission Editor, and Crusade mode.

## How does a typical match flow?

Early economy and map control → mid-game tech and [unit counters](/guides/unit-roster/) → late-game ultimate-unit showdowns — with [glory kills](/guides/glory-kills/) as constant combat punctuation. In team play, coordination around objectives (Maelstrom especially) separates good pairs from great ones — see the [co-op campaign guide](/guides/coop-campaign/).

## Is it beginner-friendly?

Yes — [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) forgive mistakes, skirmish AI scales, and the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) sequence walks your first ten hours. Veterans get their ceiling too: the [faction tier list](/guides/faction-tier-list/) tracks the launch-week meta as data arrives. Hardware-wise it is PC-only — check the official [system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/) before [December 3](/guides/release-time/).
