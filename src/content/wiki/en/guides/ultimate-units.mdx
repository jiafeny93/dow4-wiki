## What are ultimate units in Dawn of War IV?

Each of the four launch factions caps its roster with an **ultimate unit** — a super-heavy, faction-defining battlefield centerpiece that represents peak tech and can swing whole matches. Three of the four are confirmed pre-launch; the fourth (Adeptus Mechanicus) is still under wraps.

## What are the three confirmed ultimate units?

| Faction | Ultimate unit | Fantasy |
|---|---|---|
| [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) | **Imperial Knights** | Towering noble walkers with heavy weaponry |
| [Orks](/factions/orks/) | **Stompa** | A rolling green fortress of guns and teeth |
| [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) | **Awakened Monolith** | An ancient citadel that wakes up |
| [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) | TBA | Reveal pending |

## How do the ultimates compare?

Honest pre-launch answer: **flavor is confirmed, numbers are not**. Cost, build requirements, HP, damage and ability kits will decide the real tier order — and this page will carry a verified comparison table, updated per [balance patch](/guides/roadmap/), from launch week onward. Every stat on this site carries a last-verified date; we do not publish guessed numbers.

## How do you counter ultimate units?

Counterplay notes arrive with launch-week testing: what each faction's roster answers super-heavies with, from [Eliminators](/factions/space-marines/) sniper fire to Ork swarms to Necron attrition. The counter matrix will live here once verified. For per-unit stat pages across all ~64 multiplayer units, see the [complete unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/).

## Are ultimate units in every mode?

Ultimates are part of each faction's tech tree, so they appear wherever full tech is available — multiplayer, skirmish and campaign. Their role in The Last Stand survival depends on hero kits ([Last Stand guide](/guides/last-stand/)); their campaign appearances include scripted set-pieces — see the [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/) and [game modes](/guides/game-modes/).
