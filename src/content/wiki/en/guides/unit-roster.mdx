## How many units are in Dawn of War IV?

Around **64 multiplayer-relevant units** across the four launch factions, and roughly **110 units in total** once campaign-only units are included. This page is the master tracker: every unit below is officially confirmed, and individual stat pages will be linked as they are verified in-game after the December 3 unlock (Commander Edition owners start November 30 — see [release times](/guides/release-time/)).

## When do unit stats arrive?

At launch. Cost, HP, armour type, damage, abilities and upgrades for all ~64 units will be published as individual unit pages under the units category, with a last-verified date on every page. The three ultimate units — Imperial Knights, Stompa, Awakened Monolith — are compared in the [ultimate units guide](/guides/ultimate-units/). Balance patches after launch will be tracked on this page via the [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/).
