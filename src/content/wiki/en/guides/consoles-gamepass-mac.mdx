## Is Dawn of War IV on Xbox or PlayStation?

**No.** Dawn of War IV launches **December 3, 2026 on PC (Windows) via Steam only**. There are no Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, PS5 or PS4 versions — announced, rumored with sources, or otherwise. Searches for "dow 4 xbox game pass" land here because the answer matters: **not on Game Pass either**, at launch or announced.

## Will consoles ever get it?

Possibly — the honest read:

- The **creative director has publicly discussed the possibility of bringing RTS to consoles** — a genuine signal of interest, not a plan
- **Nothing is announced**: no platform, no date, no window
- RTS control schemes are the standing hurdle; a console port would need a full UI rethink

When an official announcement exists, this page carries it same-day with sources. Until then, treat every "dow 4 on console" rumor as exactly that.

## Is Dawn of War IV on Mac or Linux?

**Not officially.** The game targets Windows (see [system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/)). Two unofficial rays of hope:

- **Linux** — Closed Beta players reported the game running on Linux; unsupported, no guarantees for future updates ([Steam Deck details](/guides/steam-deck/))
- **Mac** — no reports of macOS builds or workarounds being viable; Mac players should use the cloud option below

## The confirmed alternative: GeForce NOW

**GeForce NOW support is officially confirmed for launch.** Stream the game from your Steam library through NVIDIA's cloud on almost any screen — Windows, Mac, even handhelds. It is the only verified way to play Dawn of War IV off a gaming PC today ([full breakdown](/guides/system-requirements/)).
