## Was there a Dawn of War IV beta?

**Yes — a Closed Beta ran October 21–26, 2026.** Access came through registration (the official announcement invited players to "register your interest"). The beta put a pre-launch build through its paces ahead of the [December 3 release](/guides/release-time/).

## Will there be an open beta?

**Not announced.** With the full launch now weeks away and the [Commander Edition early unlock on November 30](/guides/editions-preorder/), an open beta would be redundant on the usual publisher logic — the game itself is about to be in everyone's hands. If anything changes, our update monitor catches the announcement the same day and this page updates (it has not, as of 2026-11-18).

## What did the Closed Beta tell us?

Verified takeaways that still matter at launch:

- **Hardware continuity** — the developers confirmed: *if you ran Dawn of War IV during the Closed Beta, you should also be able to run the full game at launch* (see [system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/))
- **Linux reports** — beta players reported the game running on Linux, unofficially feeding the [Steam Deck picture](/guides/steam-deck/)
- **Stress data** — the [multiplayer modes](/guides/game-modes/) and [The Last Stand](/guides/last-stand/) got real-world load before launch

## How do I get early access now?

The Closed Beta window has passed, but early play remains available:

1. **Commander Edition** — three days early, from **November 30** ([editions compared](/guides/editions-preorder/))
2. **GeForce NOW at launch** — confirmed streaming support, no hardware barrier ([system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/))
3. Everyone else: **December 3 global launch** — full [release time guide](/guides/release-time/)

## What if I found a beta key page?

Beware of "beta key" resellers — the official registration window is closed, and third-party keys for a free closed beta are either scams or against terms. Stick to the official Steam page path documented across this wiki, from the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) to the [download size prep](/guides/download-size/).
