## How many missions does Dawn of War IV have?

**More than 70** across its four non-linear faction campaigns — every one playable solo or in [two-player co-op](/guides/coop-campaign/). The campaigns interweave into one story set on [Kronus](/guides/kronus/). See the [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/) for structure.

## How many factions are there?

**Four at launch**: [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/), [Orks](/factions/orks/), [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) and [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/). Each has its own campaign, multiplayer roster and ultimate unit ([tier list](/guides/faction-tier-list/)). Post-launch additions would follow the [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/) — nothing extra is announced.

## How many units?

Around **64 multiplayer-relevant units** at launch, roughly **110 including campaign-only units**. Track every confirmed one in the [sortable unit table](/guides/unit-roster/) — verified stats fill in the moment the game unlocks November 30.

## How many players?

- **Campaign & every mode**: 2-player co-op
- **The Last Stand survival**: 3 players, 20 waves, strike-force companions ([guide](/guides/last-stand/))
- **Competitive multiplayer**: 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 ([game modes](/guides/game-modes/))

There is no announced 4-player or larger co-op format at launch.

## How many maps?

**30 multiplayer maps** ship at launch, with free **Map Pack 1** plus more on the [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/). Map-by-map pages arrive here as they are played at launch.

## How long is the campaign?

Official hour estimates are not published — we do not print guesses. What is verifiable: 70+ missions across four storylines, non-linear structure, and [Kronus](/guides/kronus/) as the battlefield. Real completion times will be documented here from launch-week play. Meanwhile, the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) maps your first ten hours, and [release times](/guides/release-time/) marks December 3.
