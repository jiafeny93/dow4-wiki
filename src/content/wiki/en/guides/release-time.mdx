## When does Dawn of War IV release?

- **Global launch: December 3, 2026** — PC via Steam, $59.99 standard
- **Early unlock: November 30, 2026** — Commander Edition owners, three days ahead
- **Launch details reveal: November 19, 2026** — publisher Deep Silver will publish preload dates and regional unlock times
