## What hardware will big RTS battles need?

Unverified, but informed context: modern RTS games with large armies, heavy effects and 3v3 [multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/) loads tend to be CPU-bound — expect a modern 6-core CPU and 16 GB RAM to be the practical comfort zone. That is reading the genre, not a spec claim. The real numbers arrive with the official announcement.
