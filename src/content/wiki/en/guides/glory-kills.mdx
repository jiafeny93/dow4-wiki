## What are glory kills in Dawn of War IV?

Glory kills are the cinematic execution mechanic Dawn of War IV has built much of its combat identity around — finishing off weakened enemies with staged, over-the-top kill animations. They were one of the most discussed features in pre-launch coverage: the community debated what their return signals about the game's pacing, and hands-on previews put them front and center.

## How do glory kills trigger?

The pattern from pre-launch information: enemies brought low enough become execution targets, and your units finish them with a glory kill rather than a plain death. Exact health thresholds, which units can perform them, and whether they differ per faction will be verified in-game at launch and published here — this site never posts unverified numbers ([release times](/guides/release-time/)).

## What do glory kills earn you?

Reward details — resources, buffs, veterancy, morale effects — have not been fully specified pre-launch. What is known: they are woven into combat tempo rather than being pure cosmetics. Whether the reward justifies the animation time in competitive play will be one of the first things tested at launch, and this page updates with verified findings.

## Do glory kills slow down multiplayer?

The classic RTS concern: cinematic animations cost map tempo. Expect the launch-week meta analysis here to cover when to style and when to macro. For how glory kills fit the broader combat systems, see the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) and the [faction pages](/guides/factions-overview/) — all four sides, from [Space Marine](/factions/space-marines/) heroes to [Ork](/factions/orks/) swarms, interact with the mechanic.

Aesthetically, early coverage gives every faction distinct execution flavor — but "best" depends on what they *earn*, which is unverified pre-launch.
