## What should I do first in Dawn of War IV?

A proven-order checklist for your first hours:

1. **Pick a starter faction** — [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) are the classic on-ramp: durable, forgiving, strong heroes
2. **Play the campaign** — four interwoven storylines, 70+ missions, all playable [solo or in two-player co-op](/guides/co-op-guide/)
3. **Learn modes in skirmish** — up to five AI opponents and four game types before touching ranked ([game modes](/guides/game-modes/))
4. **Sample The Last Stand** — co-op survival, zero ladder anxiety ([Last Stand guide](/guides/last-stand/))
5. **Then hit multiplayer** — 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 across 30 launch maps

## Which faction should a beginner pick?

Space Marines, with [Orks](/factions/orks/) as the aggressive alternative. The full comparison — including why veterans are eyeing [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) as the most unconventional toolkit and [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) as the attrition pick — is in the [faction overview](/guides/factions-overview/).

## What systems do I need to learn?

The confirmed pillars:

- **Ultimate units** — each faction's tech-tree capstone, from Imperial Knights to the Stompa ([ultimate units](/guides/ultimate-units/))
- **Glory kills** — the signature execution mechanic woven into combat tempo ([glory kills](/guides/glory-kills/))
- **Army customization** — expanded cosmetics and army identity systems
- **Unit economics** — ~64 multiplayer units with cost/HP/armour/damage stats, all tracked post-launch in the [unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/)

## Is the campaign good for learning the game?

Yes — that is the intended path. It is non-linear, story-driven, and introduces each faction's toolkit through play. See the [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/) for how the four storylines fit together.

## When can I start, and on what hardware?

- **Launch: December 3, 2026** on PC via Steam; Commander Edition owners start **November 30** ([release times](/guides/release-time/), [editions compared](/guides/editions-preorder/))
- **Check the [system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/)** — official specs had not been published at the time of writing, and this site only posts verified numbers
- Post-launch content is already roadmap'd: two story expansions, Map Pack 1, the Mission Editor and Crusade mode ([Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/))
