## Where does Dawn of War IV take place?

**Kronus.** The publisher's official system-requirements announcement closed with: *"we look forward to welcoming you to Kronus on 3 December"* — the first direct confirmation of the campaign's battlefield. For series veterans that single name carries enormous weight; for newcomers, here is why.

## What is Kronus in Dawn of War lore?

Kronus is the planet of **Dawn of War: Dark Crusade** (2006) — widely considered the series' high-water mark. Dark Crusade built a **turn-based strategic conquest** over a territory map, where each of seven factions fought for the whole planet, with unique stronghold missions for every race. The [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) — buried under Kronus' sands — were Dark Crusade's headline addition, making their Dawn of War IV return to the same soil poetic.

## Which factions fight over Kronus this time?

Dawn of War IV launches with four playable sides, all with history on this battlefield:

| Faction | Kronus history | DoW IV role |
|---|---|---|
| [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) | Dark Crusade's Blood Ravens defense | Blood Ravens + [Dark Angels storyline](/guides/campaign-overview/) |
| [Orks](/factions/orks/) | The green tide's endless Waaagh! | Own campaign storyline |
| [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) | Awakening under Kronus — DC's icon | The [Awakened Monolith](/guides/ultimate-units/) returns |
| [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) | Mars-aligned tech claims | Unconventional newcomer kit |

## Is Dawn of War IV a Dark Crusade sequel?

Not officially called one — it is a new entry, not a remake. But the Kronus setting, the [non-linear four-faction campaign structure](/guides/campaign-overview/), and the return of [The Last Stand](/guides/last-stand/) all read as a deliberate love letter to the 2006 classic. Whether territory-conquest mechanics from Dark Crusade reappear will be verified at launch — this page updates with dated sources when it does.
