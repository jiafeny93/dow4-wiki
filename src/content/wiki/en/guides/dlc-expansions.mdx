## What DLC is planned for Dawn of War IV?

Two paid story expansions are on the published [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/):

1. **The Blood Ravens Story Prologue** — story content tied to the series' signature chapter, connecting to the [campaign's Blood Ravens storyline](/factions/space-marines/)
2. **AFTERMATH** — the second announced expansion; details still limited

## What free content is coming?

The roadmap's free track is unusually generous:

- **New modes** — additional formats beyond the [launch lineup](/guides/game-modes/)
- **Map Pack 1** — the first free map drop for [multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/)
- **Mission Editor** — create, play and **share** custom missions; the community-content play ([mods deep dive](/guides/mods/))
- **Crusade mode** — the beloved narrative army-progression mode returns in **early 2027** (moved from autumn 2026 with the launch delay)

## Are new factions DLC?

**Nothing announced.** Launch factions are the four playable sides ([overview](/guides/factions-overview/)); any roster growth would come through the roadmap's story expansions or updates — and no faction DLC has been revealed. This page updates the day anything official lands.

## Why trust this roadmap over Dawn of War 3's silence?

The fair question after DoW3's abandonment. Differences worth noting: this roadmap was **published pre-launch** (paid + free tracks itemized), the developer [KING Art](/guides/developer/) has a long support history with Iron Harvest, and the [closed beta](/guides/open-beta/) already put build quality on record. We track delivery against promise here, with dated sources, as content ships.

## When does everything arrive?

Exact dates beyond "Year One" / "early 2027" (Crusade) are not published. The [update monitor](/guides/roadmap/) catches announcements within hours — this wiki's pages update with verification dates. Launch itself: **December 3, 2026**, Commander Edition early unlock **November 30** ([release times](/guides/release-time/), [editions](/guides/editions-preorder/)).
