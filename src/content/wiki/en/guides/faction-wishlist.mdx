## Will Dawn of War IV add new factions?

**Nothing is announced.** The honest state of play: launch brings exactly [four factions](/guides/factions-overview/) — Space Marines, Orks, Necrons, Adeptus Mechanicus — and the published [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/) adds two story expansions, map packs, a Mission Editor and Crusade mode. **No faction DLC exists on the official record.** Everything below is community wishlist, labeled as such.

## Why four factions is the right launch number

Balance and asymmetry: each launch faction has a full [64-unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/), its own [campaign storyline](/guides/campaign-overview/) and a distinct identity — from [Necron attrition](/factions/necrons/) to the [Mechanicus wildcard](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/). The series' history says expansions are where factions grow ([what's actually coming](/guides/dlc-expansions/)).

## How you'll know the moment a faction is real

This wiki's [update monitor](/guides/roadmap/) catches official announcements within hours — when a fifth faction is truly announced, this page flips from wishlist to announcement coverage with sources and dates. Until then: the four-faction [tier list](/guides/faction-tier-list/) ranks what you can actually play on [December 3](/guides/release-time/).

## Wishlist ≠ promise

To be unmistakably clear (this site's rule): no Tau, no Tyranids, no Eldar, no Guard, no Gorgutz return is confirmed by anyone official. If a leaker says otherwise, treat it as fun, not news. The confirmed content pipeline lives in [DLC & expansions](/guides/dlc-expansions/).
