## How many campaigns does Dawn of War IV have?

**Four** — one per launch faction — interweaving into a single non-linear, narrative-driven story. Together they contain **more than 70 missions**. Where previous Dawn of War games gave you one perspective, Dawn of War IV makes the campaign itself a four-sided war ([factions overview](/guides/factions-overview/)).

## Which factions get campaigns?

All four launch factions:

- [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) — the Blood Ravens and Dark Angels storyline, with heroes Cyrus, Jonah Orion and Lion El'Jonson confirmed
- [Orks](/factions/orks/) — the green tide gets its own storyline, not just antagonist duty
- [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) — the ancient machine-warriors' awakening
- [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) — the machine cult's unconventional war

## What does "non-linear" mean here?

Campaign progression branches rather than running on rails: mission choice shapes the story, and the four faction campaigns interlock into one larger narrative. A mission-by-mission walkthrough section (under the missions category) goes live at launch, with verified objectives and co-op notes.

## Can I play the campaign in co-op?

Yes — the campaign and **every mode in the game** support **two-player co-op**. How invites work, difficulty scaling and what carries over are covered in the [co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/). Between story missions, The Last Stand ([guide](/guides/last-stand/)) and multiplayer ([game modes](/guides/game-modes/)) round out the package.

## How long is the campaign?

Specific hour estimates have not been verified pre-launch and we do not publish guesses. What is confirmed: 70+ missions across four campaigns, each faction storyline standing on its own. Mission count per faction and difficulty notes will be published with launch-week data.

## When does the campaign unlock?

December 3, 2026 on PC via Steam; Commander Edition owners start November 30 ([release times](/guides/release-time/)). Post-launch story content is already announced — the *Blood Ravens Story Prologue* and *AFTERMATH* expansions are on the [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/).
