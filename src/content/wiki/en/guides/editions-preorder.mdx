## What editions does Dawn of War IV have?

Pre-orders are live on Steam with at least two tiers confirmed:

| Edition | Price | Headline perk |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $59.99 | Base game, December 3 launch |
| Commander Edition | TBC | **3-day early unlock from November 30** |

The Commander Edition's full bonus list (cosmetics, DLC entitlements) had not been fully itemized at the time of writing — the Steam page is the live source, and this page updates with the verified breakdown. We do not republish unconfirmed bonus lists.

## How does the 3-day early unlock work?

Commander Edition owners start playing on **November 30, 2026** — three days ahead of the December 3 global launch. That early window matters for guides content: this site's stat pages ([unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/), [ultimate units](/guides/ultimate-units/)) begin verifying on day one of the early unlock. Full timing details, including per-region unlock times when publisher Deep Silver publishes them on **November 19**, land in the [release time guide](/guides/release-time/).

## Is Dawn of War IV on consoles?

Not at launch. Dawn of War IV releases on **PC via Steam** on December 3, 2026; the creative director has discussed the possibility of RTS on consoles, but nothing is announced. Any platform news will be added here with sources.

## Who makes Dawn of War IV?

**KING Art Games** (the studio behind Iron Harvest) develops, with **Deep Silver / PLAION** publishing under the Games Workshop license — not Relic Entertainment, which made the earlier series entries. The game was originally slated for September 17, 2026, and moved to December 3. Full post-launch plans are in the [Year One roadmap](/guides/roadmap/).

## What should a new player buy?

If you want to play the [four-faction campaign](/guides/campaign-overview/) or grind [The Last Stand](/guides/last-stand/) the moment servers open, the Commander Edition's November 30 start is the play. If you just want the full game — [multiplayer](/guides/game-modes/), [co-op everywhere](/guides/co-op-guide/) — the standard edition delivers it all on December 3. Start either way with the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/).
