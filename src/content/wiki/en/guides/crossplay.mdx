## Does Dawn of War IV have crossplay?

**No — and it does not need it yet.** Dawn of War IV launches **December 3, 2026 on PC (Windows) via Steam only**. With no console versions on store shelves, there is no other platform to cross-play with. If you and your friends are on PC, you can play everything together — see the [co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/).

## Is Dawn of War IV coming to consoles?

Not at launch. The game's creative director has publicly discussed the possibility of bringing RTS gameplay to consoles — a real signal, but **no console version has been announced**, let alone dated. When that changes, this page updates the same day with sources.

## Will crossplay be added later?

If console versions ever ship, crossplay becomes a real question — RTS cross-platform play between PC and console raises input and balance questions the developers would have to answer. Nothing is promised today. Bookmark this page: it will carry the verified answer the moment one exists.

## What about Steam Deck or handheld PC?

The game is a PC (Windows) title; official Steam Deck verification status has not been published yet — see our [system requirements](/guides/system-requirements/) page for what is confirmed, updated the moment official specs land.

## Can I play with friends on launch day?

Yes — on PC. Every mode supports **two-player co-op**, including the full campaign (70+ missions) and The Last Stand survival mode. The [co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/) covers invites and tips, [game modes](/guides/game-modes/) covers 2v2 and 3v3 team formats, and the [release time guide](/guides/release-time/) tells you exactly when you can start on December 3 (or November 30 with the [Commander Edition](/guides/editions-preorder/)).
