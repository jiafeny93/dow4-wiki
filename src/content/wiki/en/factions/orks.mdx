## What are Orks in Dawn of War IV?

Orks are one of the four playable factions at launch, and the most heavily revealed so far. Dawn of War IV leans into classic Ork fantasy: endless green waves, ramshackle machinery, beasts straight out of the jungle, and a comedy-tipped power spike when the biggest walkers hit the field. For how they stack against the other sides, see the [faction overview](/guides/factions-overview/).

## Which Ork units are confirmed?

Orks currently have the most named pre-launch roster:

| Unit | Battlefield role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Boyz | Core melee infantry | The green tide's backbone |
| Beast Snagga Boyz | Beast-hunting melee | Aggressive anti-monster line |
| Bomb Squigs | Living ordnance | Explosive beast units |
| Squig-Hog Riders | Fast cavalry | Mobile shock harassment |
| Stompa | **Ultimate unit** | Massive Ork super-walker |

More reveals are landing steadily — the cross-faction list lives in our [complete unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/), where cost, HP and damage stats will be filled in and verified the moment the game unlocks.

## How do Orks play?

Pre-launch information points to Orks as the **aggression faction**: cheap bodies, early map pressure, and asymmetric tools (bomb-carrying beasts, squig cavalry) that let experienced players dictate tempo. Their ceiling is the Stompa — one of the three faction ultimate units compared in our [ultimate units guide](/guides/ultimate-units/), alongside the Space Marines' Imperial Knights and the Necrons' Awakened Monolith.

## Do Orks appear in the campaign?

Yes. Dawn of War IV ships four non-linear campaigns — one per faction — with 70+ missions total. The Ork campaign gives the faction its own storyline rather than casting them purely as antagonists. The full structure is broken down in the [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/), and everything is playable in two-player co-op (see the [co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/)).

## Should I main Orks?

Pick Orks if you want:

- Relentless early aggression and map control
- Units with personality — beasts, riders, walking bombs
- A swarm style that punishes passive opponents

Consider [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/) for a durable elite style, [Necrons](/factions/necrons/) for attrition and reanimation, or [Adeptus Mechanicus](/factions/adeptus-mechanicus/) for the strangest mechanics in the game.

## When can I play them?

December 3, 2026 on PC, or November 30 with the Commander Edition's three-day early unlock — see the [release time guide](/guides/release-time/) for the full schedule.
