## What is Adeptus Mechanicus in Dawn of War IV?

Adeptus Mechanicus — the Imperium's cult of machine-worshipping tech-priests — is one of the four playable launch factions. It is also the faction generating the most curiosity among RTS players, because hands-on previews have singled it out: **Adeptus Mechanicus plays very differently from the other three factions**, with mechanics that break the standard RTS template.

## How is Adeptus Mechanicus different?

That is the million-credit question pre-launch. Previews confirm the *degree* of difference — this is the faction veteran players will need to relearn the game for — but the specific systems have not been fully detailed yet. What we know for certain:

- It is one of four launch factions with its own campaign storyline
- Preview coverage describes its playstyle as very different from [Space Marines](/factions/space-marines/), [Orks](/factions/orks/) and [Necrons](/factions/necrons/)
- Full faction mechanics will be documented here the week of launch, verified in-game

This page updates the moment the game unlocks — see the [release time guide](/guides/release-time/) for exactly when.

## Which Adeptus Mechanicus units are confirmed?

The unit roster is still under reveal. As with all faction pages on this site, we only list confirmed units — no speculation, no lore-guesses presented as fact. The master list lives in the [complete unit roster](/guides/unit-roster/), covering all ~64 multiplayer-relevant units across the four factions, and Mechanicus unit pages will be published with verified stats after the December 3 unlock.

## Should I main Adeptus Mechanicus?

Pick Adeptus Mechanicus if you want:

- The most mechanically unusual faction in the game
- A fresh template instead of familiar RTS archetypes
- Deep tech-tree play with unusual army construction

For a comparison against all four sides — including who beginners should actually pick first — see the [faction overview](/guides/factions-overview/) and the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/).

Each faction has its own non-linear campaign within the 70+ mission structure, playable solo or in two-player co-op. Details in the [campaign guide](/guides/campaign-overview/) and [co-op guide](/guides/co-op-guide/).
